# 2026-10-10 — DEBT-054 — The fund payment auto-match spec against its code: what the release check found

**Found by:** spec-checker, release preparation of 2026-10-10 (read in the code, not run); completes DEBT-049

**Where:** `docs/spec/fund-payment-auto-match.md`, `src-tauri/src/use_cases/fund_payment_reconciliation/` (`parsing/pdf_parser.rs`, `output/pdf_candidate_mapper.rs`, `core/processor.rs`, `reconciliation/reconciliation_pass.rs`, `orchestrator.rs`), `src/features/fund-payment-match/pdf_data_table/PdfDataTable.tsx`, `src/features/fund-payment-match/gateway.test.ts`

**Observation:** 32 of 39 rules are fully implemented and 27 have a test of their own. It was this check that showed the parser could not read a negative total line (fixed the same day, PR #231). Still open: (1) FPA-020 and FPA-030 — the warning for a total that does not match its lines, and the count and samples of unparsed lines, are shown only by `PdfDataTable`, which nothing renders: the user sees neither. An unread total line is exactly such an unparsed line, and it stayed invisible in production. (2) `pdf_candidate_mapper.rs` gives a candidate a total of 0 when a line's fund is not its group's: nobody can validate such a candidate; FPA-075 now rejects it before any write, but nothing says why it exists. (3) FPA-040 — a negative line can be matched to a procedure of the same patient and date with an amount mismatch, where the rule says it is always "not found"; no test feeds a negative line through the matching. (4) FPA-150 flags a line as "too many" above 8 candidates even when one has the exact amount. (5) FPA-240 — "linked once" is not enforced in the backend. (6) FPA-025 — the short date form is rejected. No test: FPA-210, 220, 250 (the backend application of the correction), FPA-310 (the mismatch path), FPA-440; for the rules of this batch, the content of the FPA-071 notice, the date-range clause of FPA-070, and comments that cite FPA-070 where FPA-071 or FPA-072 is meant. `gateway.test.ts` builds a response without `left_out_groups`.
